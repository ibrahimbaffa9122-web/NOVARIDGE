AGREEMENT SUMMARY

Understand the terms
before participating.

These summaries highlight important provisions. The signed agreement is the controlling document.

SECTION 02

Islamic financial principles

The arrangement is intended to follow Mudarabah: the Company provides capital and the Trader contributes skill and effort. No fixed return or guaranteed profit is promised. Profit is shared by an agreed percentage.

Genuine loss from ordinary market movement is borne by the Company as capital provider. A Trader is financially responsible only for loss attributable to proven negligence, misconduct, dishonesty or breach of agreement.

SECTION 03

Permitted trading activity

Trading is limited to approved cryptocurrency spot transactions involving assets accepted by the Company as permissible. Futures, perpetual contracts, options, margin, leverage, borrowing, interest-based products and prohibited assets are not allowed.

SECTION 04

Profit and loss allocation

Realised net profit is distributed weekly at 60% to the Trader and 40% to the Company, after eligible fees and applicable loss recovery. No profit is due when no profit is realised.

Ordinary market losses are borne by the capital provider, except where the Trader's proven misconduct, negligence or breach caused the loss.

SECTION 05

Trader responsibilities

The Trader must follow agreed risk limits, keep accurate records, protect account credentials, use authorised accounts and wallets, and act honestly and diligently at all times.

SECTION 06

Reporting and account loss

The Trader must provide weekly trading summaries and promptly report unusual losses, suspected compromise, unauthorised access or any event that may affect the capital.

SECTION 07

Duration and termination

The agreement may run for 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 weeks, as selected and signed by both parties. Early termination, settlement and return of remaining capital are handled under the signed agreement.

SECTION 08

Privacy and dispute resolution

Parties must protect confidential information and handle personal data in line with applicable Nigerian law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Disputes should first be addressed through good-faith discussion, followed by the agreed legal process.
